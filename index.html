How can I find my signature scent?

Tell us the notes, places, or memories you’re drawn to. Our fragrance team will help you find a scent that feels like you.

Where is my order?

Once your order ships, you’ll receive a tracking link by email. If you need a hand, include your order number in your message.

Can you help me choose a gift?

Of course. Share a little about who you’re shopping for and we’ll suggest a thoughtful fragrance or discovery set.

When will I hear back?

We aim to reply within one business day, Monday through Friday. During especially busy moments, it may take a little longer.
