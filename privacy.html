Privacy policy

Contact and signup forms

The contact and email signup forms are demonstrations. With JavaScript enabled, submitting either form displays a preview message without sending your entries to AK&CO. If you call us directly, we use the information you choose to share to respond to your enquiry.

Browser storage

The shop uses session storage to remember whether the email signup prompt has appeared in your browser tab. That setting stores whether the prompt has appeared and contains no email address.

The shopping bag stores your selected fragrances, sizes, prices, and quantities in local storage so they stay available when you return in the same browser. You can remove items from the bag or clear the site’s browser data.

External services

The site loads some images from external services. The contact page also embeds Google Maps. When your browser requests those resources, the providers may receive technical information such as your IP address.
